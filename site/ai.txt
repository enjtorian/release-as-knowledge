# Scope

This policy applies to all content served from https://releaseasknowledge.com,
including but not limited to: the manifesto, the eight principles, the four
level definitions (L1 Identify / L2 Trust / L3 Understand / L4 Share), the
Mode A / Mode B descriptions, and all curated reading-list entries owned by
this site (external articles linked from the reading list are governed by
their own publishers' policies).
